Power BI report - “Market Intelligence & Demand Analytics" 

Data Source: The sample data used in this report was prepared based on research and analysis of publicly available information on the internet, and used in Excel format as the source for the Power BI report.
Report Structure:
The report consists of 3 pages, with navigation buttons added on each page for easy movement between them:
Page 1 – Market Intelligence Dashboard
Page 2 – Apple Products Insights
Page 3 – Details

I've built this as category-wise analytics rather than a single overall comparison, since a brand can have many items under it, and the top brand isn't necessarily the same as the top-selling model. As a default view, I've covered the Mobile category across brands, models, and countries, but the same structure can be extended to other categories like laptops, TVs, etc.
Key highlights of the report:
•	All visuals are cross-filterable, with slicers for Category, Month, Country, and Brand.
•	Added drill-through to a Details page, allowing you to click into any visual for more granular insights.
•	For the Monthly Performance visual, I've added a toggle button to switch between two fields (Brand/Model or Category/Model).
•	For the Market Share Distribution visual, I've added drill-down functionality (Brand/Model or Category/Model).
•	As requested, I've also built a dedicated "Apple Product Market Insights & Analytics" dashboard with its own drill-through details view, breaking down Apple's performance by category (Mobile, Tablet, Laptop, Watch, Earbuds) instead of by brand.
•	Included navigation buttons for smooth movement between pages.

Market Intelligence & Demand Analytics — Default View: Mobile Category
Key insights:
Market Leaders
•	Apple leads the Mobile category with 42.12% market share, followed by Samsung at 37.01% and Xiaomi at 20.87%. 
•	Galaxy A56 is the leading model with 5.51% market share, while Singapore is the leading country with 5.29% market share. 
Monthly Performance
•	Tracked monthly performance for Apple, Samsung, and Xiaomi from October 2025 through September 2026. 
•	Apple and Samsung show noticeable month-to-month fluctuations, while Xiaomi remains comparatively lower and more stable across the period. 
Market Demand Distribution by Country
•	Visualized market demand distribution across 18 countries, highlighting the geographic concentration of Mobile category demand. 
•	Singapore represents the largest share among the countries shown. 
Top Selling Models
•	Galaxy A56 leads with 71M units sold, followed by iPhone 16 Pro at 71M and iPhone 15 Pro at 70M. 
•	Several other models are closely grouped in the 65M–69M range, indicating relatively balanced demand among the leading models. 
MoM Net Revenue
•	Monthly net revenue remained relatively consistent, ranging from approximately 125K to 148K during the reporting period. 
•	Revenue peaked at approximately 148K in August 2026, with the lowest point at approximately 125K in April 2026. 

Apple Product Market Insights & Analytics
Key insights:
•	Mobile is Apple's leading category with 47.53% market share, followed by Tablet at 17.02%, Laptop at 11.98%, Watch at 11.90%, and Earbuds at 11.57%. 
•	iPhone 16 Pro is the leading model with 6.15% market share, while Australia is the leading country with 5.30% market share. 
•	Among the top-selling Apple products, iPhone 16 Pro leads with 71M units, followed by iPhone 15 Pro at 70M and MacBook Air M4 at 70M. 
•	Other leading products, including iPhone 15, iPhone 15 Pro Max, iPhone 15 Plus, Watch Series 10, and Watch Ultra 2, are within the 68M–69M range, showing relatively balanced demand across Apple's major product lines. 
•	Monthly net revenue remained within approximately 116K–127K, with the highest value of 127K in October 2025 and the lowest of 116K in April 2026.
