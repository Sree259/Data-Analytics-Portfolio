Tableau Report – Road Accidents Dashboard

Data
Source:
Road accident data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Tableau dashboard. The dataset includes accident severity, casualty details, vehicle type, weather conditions, road surface, road type, location, and year.
Report Structure
The dashboard provides an interactive view of road accidents and casualties, with year-over-year comparison and analysis of serious casualties across different categories.
Key Highlights
•	Interactive filters for Current Year, Previous Year, and Accident Severity. 
•	KPI cards showing Total Accidents, Total Casualties, Fatal, Serious, and Slight Casualties, with YoY changes. 
•	Serious Casualties by Vehicle Type to compare casualty levels across vehicle categories. 
•	Weather and Road Surface analysis showing the conditions associated with serious casualties. 
•	Road Type analysis comparing single carriageways, dual carriageways, roundabouts, and other road types. 
•	Geographical Map showing the distribution of serious casualties by location. 

Key Insights
Accident & Casualty Overview
•	Approximately 144.4K accidents and 195.7K casualties are recorded for the selected year. 
•	Serious casualties account for approximately 27.0K, while fatal casualties account for approximately 2.9K. 
•	YoY indicators provide a quick view of changes in accident and casualty levels. 
Road & Environmental Analysis
•	Fine weather accounts for the majority of serious casualties. 
•	Dry road surfaces represent the largest share of serious casualties. 
•	Single carriageways account for approximately 78.5% of serious casualties, followed by dual carriageways at approximately 15.0%. 
Geographical Analysis
•	The map highlights the regional distribution of serious casualties, helping identify areas with higher concentrations. 

Overall Purpose
The Road Accidents Dashboard provides a consolidated view of accident and casualty performance, helping stakeholders analyze YoY trends, severity, vehicle types, road and weather conditions, road types, and geographical patterns to identify key road safety trends.
