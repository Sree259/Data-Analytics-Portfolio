Tableau Report – Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard

Data
Source:
Employee workforce data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Tableau dashboard. The dataset includes employee demographics, department, education field, job role, gender, age group, job satisfaction, and attrition information.
Report Structure
The dashboard provides an interactive view of employee workforce composition and attrition, with analysis across departments, age groups, gender, education fields, job roles, and satisfaction levels.
Key Highlights
•	KPI cards showing Employee Count, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Active Employees, and Average Age. 
•	Attrition by Gender comparing male and female employee attrition. 
•	Department-wise Attrition showing attrition distribution across departments. 
•	Employee Count by Age Group highlighting workforce distribution across different age ranges. 
•	Job Satisfaction Rating by job role and satisfaction level. 
•	Education Field-wise Attrition identifying fields with higher attrition counts. 
•	Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group providing a combined demographic view. 

Key Insights
Workforce Overview
•	The dashboard covers approximately 1,470 employees, with 237 attritions and an overall attrition rate of approximately 16.12%. 
•	Approximately 1,233 employees remain active, with an average employee age of 37 years. 
Attrition Analysis
•	Attrition varies across departments, gender, education fields, and age groups. 
•	The job satisfaction view highlights differences in satisfaction levels across various job roles. 
•	Age-group analysis provides visibility into workforce composition and attrition patterns across different employee segments. 

Overall Purpose
The Employee Attrition & Workforce Analytics Dashboard provides a consolidated view of employee headcount, attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, departments, and education fields, helping stakeholders identify workforce patterns and areas requiring further HR analysis.
