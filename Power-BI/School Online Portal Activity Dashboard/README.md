Power BI Report – School Online Portal Activity Dashboard

Data
Source: School online portal activity data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Power BI report.
Report Structure
The report provides an interactive view of student and teacher portal activity, covering user engagement, onboarding, login activity, and school-level performance.
Key Highlights
•	Interactive filters for School Name and Date. 
•	KPI cards showing Total Students, Average Active Students, Students Onboard, Billable Students, Total Teachers, Average Active Teachers, and Teachers Onboard. 
•	Active Users Line Trend comparing student and teacher activity over time. 
•	School Portal Login Percentage comparing student and teacher login activity across schools. 
•	Average Active Users by School to identify schools with higher levels of student and teacher engagement. 
•	Detailed Student Details table containing school, city, grade, section, student ID, record date, presence, last login, special needs, and onboarding dates. 
•	Toggle buttons for Student and Teacher views to support focused analysis. 

Key Insights
Overall User Activity
•	The dashboard tracks approximately 5.2K students and 173 teachers. 
•	Average active users include approximately 477 students and 43 teachers. 
•	Approximately 1.87K students are onboarded, with the same number shown as billable students. 
•	107 teachers are onboarded. 
User Engagement
•	Student and teacher activity varies throughout the reporting period, with visible fluctuations in daily active users. 
•	The login comparison highlights differences in student and teacher portal engagement across schools. 
•	Average Active Users by School provides a school-level view of engagement and helps identify schools with higher portal activity. 
Student Details
The detailed section provides record-level information that can be used to investigate student attendance/activity, login status, onboarding dates, school, grade, section, and other student attributes.
Overall Purpose
School Online Portal Activity Dashboard provides a consolidated view of student and teacher engagement, onboarding, login activity, and school-level portal usage, enabling stakeholders to monitor adoption and identify variations in online activity across schools.
