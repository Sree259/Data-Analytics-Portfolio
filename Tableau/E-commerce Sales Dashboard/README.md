Tableau Report – E-commerce Sales Dashboard

Data
Source:
E-commerce sales data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Tableau dashboard. The dataset includes sales, profit, order quantity, product categories, markets, customer segments, and monthly performance.
Report Structure
The dashboard provides an interactive view of YTD sales performance, comparing current-year results with the previous year across categories, markets, and customer segments.
Key Highlights
•	Interactive filters for Market and Customer Segment. 
•	KPI cards showing Sales, Profit, and Order Quantity, with comparison against the previous year. 
•	Market Share by Sales showing sales contribution across different regions. 
•	Category-wise YTD vs PYTD Sales highlighting category performance and YoY changes. 
•	Market-wise Customer Acquisition per Month showing monthly customer acquisition trends across markets. 

Key Insights
Sales Performance
•	The dashboard tracks approximately $1.30M in sales and 157.9K orders for the selected view. 
•	Sales and order quantity show year-over-year changes, while profit provides an additional measure of financial performance. 
Category & Market Analysis
•	Category-wise analysis highlights products with positive and negative YoY sales changes. 
•	Europe and LATAM represent significant portions of the overall sales contribution. 
•	Monthly customer acquisition trends vary across markets, providing visibility into regional customer growth. 

Overall Purpose
The E-commerce Sales Dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales, profitability, order quantity, category performance, market contribution, and customer acquisition, helping stakeholders monitor YTD performance and identify changes across markets and product categories.
