Power BI Report – Car Sales Insights & Analytics

Data
Source: Sample car sales data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Power BI report.

Report Structure
The report consists of 2 pages, with navigation buttons for easy movement between pages:
Page 1 – Car Sales Dashboard
Page 2 – Car Sales Details

Car Sales Insights & Analytics provides a consolidated view of automotive sales performance, helping users analyze revenue, sales volume, pricing, model performance, customer demand, and company contribution through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

Key Highlights
•	Interactive date range slicer with filters for Body Style, Dealer Name, Transmission, and Engine. 
•	KPI cards showing YTD Total Sales, YTD Average Price, and YTD Cars Sold, along with period comparisons. 
•	Weekly Sales Trend to identify sales fluctuations and peak-performing periods. 
•	Sales by Body Style and Color to understand vehicle demand patterns. 
•	Top-Selling Models highlighting models with the highest sales contribution. 
•	Company Performance table comparing YTD average price, cars sold, total sales, and percentage contribution. 
•	Cross-filtering across visuals enables users to explore sales performance dynamically. 
•	Navigation buttons provide easy movement between the dashboard and detailed transaction view.

Car Sales Details
The page provides transaction-level information including Car ID, Date, Customer, Dealer, Company, Colour, Model, and Total Sales. Users can apply the available filters to investigate individual transactions and validate the KPIs and trends presented on the main dashboard.
