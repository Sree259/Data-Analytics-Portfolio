Power BI Report – Flight Operational Dashboard

Data
Source: Flight operational data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Power BI report.
Report Structure
The report consists of 2 pages, with navigation between the summary dashboard and detailed flight-level information:
Page 1 – Flight Operational Dashboard
Page 2 – Flight Operational Details
The report provides an overview of flight performance, delays, passenger volumes, airport performance, and irregular operations, with the ability to drill into individual flight records.

Key Highlights
•	Interactive Departure Date slicer with From Airport and To Airport filters. 
•	KPI cards showing Total Flights, On-Time Flights, Delayed Flights, Booked Passengers, Boarded Passengers, and No-Show Passengers. 
•	On-Time Performance (OTP) gauge to monitor overall flight punctuality. 
•	Average Delay Minutes trend to identify changes in operational performance. 
•	Irregular Operations analysis covering cancellations, ramp returns, diversions, air returns, and re-routes. 
•	Airport Performance comparison with a toggle between OTP and Average Delay Minutes. 
•	OTP trend analysis by year, quarter, month, and day to identify operational patterns. 
•	Detailed flight-level page for investigating individual flight performance. 

Key Insights 
Flight Operational Dashboard
Overall Performance
•	52K total flights were operated during the selected period. 
•	34K flights were on time, resulting in an overall OTP of 66%. 
•	Approximately 18K flights experienced delays. 
•	Around 7M passengers were booked, with approximately 6M passengers boarded. 
•	Approximately 201K passengers were recorded as no-shows. 
•	The average delay was approximately 39.07 minutes. 
Airport & Operational Performance
•	Airport-level performance can be compared using OTP and Average Delay Minutes, helping identify airports with different operational performance patterns. 
•	The dashboard highlights UGC as the leading airport in the displayed performance comparison. 
•	Cancellations represent the largest category of irregular operations, followed by ramp returns, diversions, air returns, and re-routes. 
OTP Trend
•	OTP varies across the reporting period, with noticeable fluctuations during the early months. 
•	Performance generally improves through parts of the later period, while still showing daily and monthly variations. 
•	The trend view allows users to analyze performance at different time levels, from year and quarter down to individual days.

Flight Operational Details
The  page provides detailed records including Departure Date, Flight Number, From/To Airport, STD, ATD, STA, ATA, Booked Passengers, Boarded Passengers, No-Show Passengers, Delay Minutes, and On-Time/Delay status.
This page supports flight-level investigation and validation, allowing users to identify specific delayed flights, compare scheduled versus actual timings, and analyze passenger and operational performance.
