Tableau Report – Sales Dashboard | Overview

Data
Source:
Sales transaction data prepared in Excel and used as the source for the Tableau dashboard. The dataset includes sales, profit, quantity, customer segments, locations, regions, and monthly sales information.
Report Structure
The dashboard provides an overview of sales and profitability performance, with year-over-year comparison and analysis by segment, location, region, and customer.
Key Highlights
•	KPI cards showing Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity with CY vs PY comparison. 
•	Sales and Profit by Location providing a geographical view of performance. 
•	Monthly Sales by Segment comparing Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office performance. 
•	Sales Average and Profit Average visualizations showing geographical distribution. 
•	Total Sales by Location and Region highlighting regional and customer-level sales performance. 

Key Insights
Overall Performance
•	Total sales are approximately $733K, with profit of approximately $93K in the selected period. 
•	Sales, profit, and quantity KPIs include year-over-year percentage comparisons. 
Segment & Regional Analysis
•	Monthly trends show variations in sales across Consumer, Corporate, and Home Office segments. 
•	Regional analysis highlights sales contribution across East, West, South, and Central regions. 
•	Location-level analysis helps identify customers and areas contributing significantly to overall sales. 

Overall Purpose
The Sales Dashboard | Overview provides a consolidated view of sales, profit, quantity, customer segments, locations, and regional performance, enabling stakeholders to monitor trends and identify key areas contributing to overall business performance.
